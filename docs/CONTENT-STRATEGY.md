## Editorial categories

1. Fresh seasonal produce — selection, storage and seasonal availability.
2. Recipes with local ingredients — adaptable, everyday meal ideas.
3. Saving money on groceries — planning, portions, pantry management; avoid unsupported savings percentages.
4. Family and everyday food — practical shopping routines without health claims.
5. Ramadan and seasonal shopping — logistics and meal planning; avoid assuming a religious audience in the main brand voice.
6. Kulha! news — product updates, coverage announcements and shop onboarding, published only when confirmed.

## Article page template

- Breadcrumb and category label
- H1, lead, author / editorial owner and publication date
- Estimated reading time, set from final text length
- Table of contents for longer stories
- Article body with descriptive headings and sourced claims
- Share links and author note
- Related stories
- Contextual Kulha! CTA

Do not display a date or author identity until one is confirmed. No fake bylines or invented sources.
