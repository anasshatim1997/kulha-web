# Native-speaker and launch review checklist

The Darija copy is working product copy, not a claim of universal regional wording. Review it with paid native reviewers from the cities and neighbourhoods actually targeted at launch.

## Darija Arabic and Arabizi

- Check each Arabic-script sentence for natural Moroccan phrasing, spelling, agreement and punctuation.
- Check Arabizi forms and numerals (3, 7, 9) against the preferred community spelling; there is no single official transliteration.
- Test whether "Kulha!" is heard as a playful grocery / food name or as a confusing imperative or feminine "all of it" form.
- Check the pronunciation and associations with speakers from Casablanca, Rabat-Salé, Fès, Marrakech, Tangier / Tetouan and the first actual launch cities.
- Ask Amazigh-language speakers from relevant communities to flag phonetic overlap, awkward readings or negative associations. Do not generalize from one Tamazight, Tarifit or Tashelhit review to all Amazigh languages.
- Avoid mixing Darija, MSA, Egyptian and Gulf forms. Keep formal legal content in reviewed French / Arabic rather than casual Darija.

## Cultural and visual review

- Confirm that the Moroccan references feel contemporary and specific without caricature.
- Review the use of zellige-inspired geometry, market imagery and Arabic lettering; use only if culturally and visually appropriate.
- Avoid implying all Moroccan shoppers share one language, diet, city, family structure or religious practice.

## Product and legal review

- Replace every launch placeholder with confirmed operational data before making it public.
- Verify +212 number normalization and all phone examples with the backend team.
- Test Arabic RTL, Arabizi LTR, French text expansion and VoiceOver / TalkBack output.
- Confirm legal entity, privacy responsibilities, payment processor, refunds, partner terms and food-handling claims with qualified advisers.
- Verify domain, trademark, social handles and iOS / Android store names for Kulha! before marketing spend.
